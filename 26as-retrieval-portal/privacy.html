Privacy Policy

Form 26AS PDF Exporter Companion Extension • Last Updated: September 2026
🔒 Zero-Data Collection Guarantee The Form 26AS PDF Exporter Companion extension does not collect, record, transmit, or monetize any personal, financial, or authentication data. All document processing and PDF generation occurs strictly client-side on your local device.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy outlines how the Form 26AS PDF Exporter Companion Chrome extension handles data when you interact with it. Our core architectural design ensures that all sensitive tax and credential data remains exclusively on your machine.

2. Data Collection and Usage

  • Zero Personal Data Stored: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or mailing addresses.
  • Zero Financial or Tax Data Stored: Taxpayer statements, Form 26AS records, PAN numbers, and TDS amounts are processed exclusively in your browser's temporary memory (RAM) to render the PDF file. No data is written to external databases, remote servers, or persistent tracking logs.
  • Zero Credentials Stored: The extension does not store, log, or transmit passwords or authentication tokens.

3. Chrome Permissions Justification

The extension requests the minimum necessary permissions to fulfill its single purpose:

  • downloads: Required solely to save the locally generated Form 26AS PDF document directly into your browser's Downloads folder.
  • offscreen: Required to launch a background sandboxed iframe for client-side PDF document vector rendering using pdfMake.

4. Third-Party Sharing and Tracking

  • We do not sell, license, or transfer user data to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.
  • The extension contains zero advertising scripts, analytics SDKs, or tracking pixels.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to maintain compliance with Chrome Web Store developer policies. Any updates will be reflected directly on this page with a revised date.
